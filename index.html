POLICIES

Last updated 16 October 2026. ODOS is an online clothing and lifestyle store based in Egypt. Questions about anything here: odostore11@gmail.com.

Privacy Cookies and storage Terms Refunds and exchanges

Privacy policy

What we collect. When you place an order we collect your name, email, phone number, delivery address, city, the payment method you choose and any notes you add. Your WhatsApp number is optional. When you contact us we collect your name, email and message. If you join the newsletter or create an account we collect your email.

Why we use it. To confirm, deliver and support your order, to reply to your messages, to send your order confirmation, and, if you signed up, to send you emails and your discount code. We do not sell your data.

Who handles it. We use a few services to run the shop: Supabase (accounts, carts, wishlists and order tracking), Resend (order confirmation emails and delivering your order and messages to us), Cloudflare (hosting, image delivery, analytics and receiving your order form) and Google Fonts. These providers process data on our behalf. Payments are sent by you directly through Telda, InstaPay or Vodafone Cash, or paid in cash on delivery. We do not see or store card numbers.

How long we keep it. We keep order records as long as we need them to fulfil your order, handle returns and meet our legal and accounting duties. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights. You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop sending you emails, by writing to odostore11@gmail.com. Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) gives you these rights.

Cookies and storage

ODOS does not use advertising or marketing cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

To make the shop work, your browser stores a few items on your device: your cart, wishlist, recently viewed products, the order numbers you placed on this device, a discount code if you applied one, and whether you have dismissed our notices. If you sign in, your cart and wishlist are also saved to your account. You can clear this any time in your browser settings. Doing so empties your cart and wishlist on this device.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you open the site.

Terms and conditions

Orders. Placing an order is a request to buy. We confirm each order with you by phone, WhatsApp or email before it ships. Prices are in Egyptian pounds (EGP). Items are subject to stock.

Payment. You can pay by Telda, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash or cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available in every governorate in Egypt.

Delivery. Delivery is free across Egypt. Orders usually arrive within 3 to 5 business days after confirmation. We do not ship outside Egypt yet.

Products. We show photos of the actual products. Colors can look slightly different depending on your screen.

Governing law. These terms are governed by the laws of Egypt.

Refunds and exchanges

Time limit. Contact us within 7 days of receiving your order, with your order number, by email at odostore11@gmail.com, on WhatsApp at 01556059711, or through the contact form.

Exchanges. We can exchange a size, subject to stock.

Refunds. You can ask for a refund instead of an exchange. Items should be unused and in their original condition. If your item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, we will replace it or refund you.

How refunds are paid. We refund to the payment method you used, or to another method we agree with you. We confirm the details, including return shipping, when you contact us.

THE PATH & THE MOMENT

01

What's ODOS?

Odos is Greek for "path". It's the one we're all on: walking, running, sometimes just crawling through it. Nobody's really ahead of anybody. We're all just trying to figure ourselves out along the way.

What's KAIROS then?

Seize.
