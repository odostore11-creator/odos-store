WHY "ODOS"

Odos is Greek for "path" — the one we're all on, walking, running, sometimes just crawling through it. Nobody's really ahead of anybody. We're all just trying to figure ourselves out along the way. Execute.

Kairos is Greek for "opportunity." It's not something you plan for — it just shows up. A moment, an opening, a shot you didn't see coming. You either take it and let it bend your path somewhere new, or it slips by and you keep walking the same way you were. Seize.

Whatever you're trying to surpass or achieve — a final, a certain GPA, your dream job, a shot you've been waiting on — wear it. Every time you put it on, you remember why you started. Remember.
